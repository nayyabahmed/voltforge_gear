# Tools

Every tool the handbook uses, when it is first needed, and what to do if you
do not have it. You do NOT need everything on day one - tools are acquired
progressively, like everything else in this project (see
[the guiding principles](style-guides-principles/GUIDING-PRINCIPLES.md)).

> **Nothing in Part 1 needs a tool you cannot improvise.** The first genuinely
> required purchase arrives in Part 2, and even the 3D printer has
> printer-free routes through every activity.

Anything marked 🔒 has rules in [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md) and is adult-supervised.

## What to Buy First, If You Are Buying

Topic 4.1's spend-progressively rule applied to tools:

1. **Safety glasses.** Nothing else on this list is a prerequisite for them.
2. **A ruler with clear mm markings**, then calipers when Topic 1.5 makes you
   want them.
3. **Screwdrivers and hex drivers of the correct sizes** - the single most
   common cause of a damaged donor part is the wrong driver.
4. **Soldering iron with fume extraction**, before Part 3's real electronics.
5. **LiPo-safe charging equipment**, before any LiPo pack enters the house.

Everything else can wait for the topic that needs it.

## Maintenance Rules

1. Add a tool the moment a topic first requires it, with the topic number.
2. Always record a fallback for readers who do not own the tool.
3. Safety-relevant tools must link to their rules in [SAFETY.md](SAFETY.md).
4. If a tool has no honest fallback, say so plainly rather than inventing a
   weak one - safety glasses and the correct charger have no substitute.
